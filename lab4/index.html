Lab 4: Cleaning Web Data for Visualization

Short Analysis

This visualization analyzes a dataset of 1,200 tweets curated from CardiffNLP’s TweetEval benchmark across diverse topics (Sports, News, Tech, Gaming, and General). Key cleaning steps included dropping missing records, removing duplicate tweet IDs, sanitizing text whitespaces, stripping leading "@" symbols from usernames, and converting timestamp strings into structured temporal attributes (date, hour, and weekday). Sentiment was calculated using the pre-trained CardiffNLP Twitter-RoBERTa model fine-tuned on social media text, deriving a continuous score as P(Positive) - P(Negative). The visualization encodes tweet creation time on the horizontal axis and continuous sentiment on the vertical axis, while bubble radius represents retweet volume and color denotes the predicted sentiment category. The observed pattern shows that gaming and technology discussions cluster predominantly within positive and neutral territory, whereas high-retweet outliers skew significantly negative, demonstrating how viral engagement often peaks around breaking controversies and sports events.
