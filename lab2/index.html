Multivariate Lollipop Chart: City Demographics & Climate

A multi-channel D3 visualization encoding population, temperature, development level, and geographical region.

Design Justification

To avoid the visual clutter of standard scatter plots, this visualization uses a sorted Lollipop Chart. The 4 required dimensions are mapped as follows:

  • Population (Ratio): Mapped to the horizontal length (X-axis position), providing the most accurate visual channel for judging quantitative differences.
  • Region (Nominal): Mapped to the vertical categorical grouping (Y-axis) and sorted, grouping cities geographically to highlight intra-regional comparisons.
  • Temperature (Interval): Encoded using a diverging/sequential color scale (blue for colder climates to deep red/orange for hotter climates), which intuitively matches real-world temperature perceptions.
  • Development Level (Ordinal): Encoded via circle point size (radius) (Low = 5px, Medium = 8px, High = 11px), reflecting the ordered progression of development.

Viewers can readily observe that southern cities exhibit significantly higher average temperatures alongside lower population sizes, while northern and western hubs generally demonstrate higher development levels and larger metropolitan populations.
